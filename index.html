Before we start

A few things
you might ask.

What do I need to prepare?

Your business name, logo if you have one, services or products, photos and contact details. A few example websites and a rough goal help establish the direction.

Can I use my own domain and hosting?

Yes. The website can be prepared for your domain and hosting. The right setup depends on the features you need, and can be discussed before the project starts.

How much will a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, content and functionality. Tell me what you have in mind and I can suggest a scope and quote before any work begins.

Can you add WhatsApp or enquiry links?

Yes. Contact buttons can link to your business WhatsApp, email or other enquiry channels. Booking and payment integrations can be discussed as part of the scope.
